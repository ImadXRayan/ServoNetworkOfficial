# Servo Coin (SVO) Tokenomics

Servo Coin (SVO) is the native utility token of the Servo Network ecosystem, deployed on the Polygon blockchain.

## Token Details
- **Token Name:** Servo Coin
- **Ticker:** SVO
- **Network:** Polygon (ERC-20)
- **Contract Address:** `0xD13BeA2028127a6067ea4CdDE4F6EaA243B82af8`

## Token Utility
1. **Marketplace Payments:** SVO is the primary currency for purchasing services on the Servo Network.
2. **Escrow System:** Funds are locked in SVO during service delivery to ensure fairness and trust.
3. **Staking:** Users can stake SVO to earn rewards and participate in the network's security and growth.
4. **Governance:** SVO holders will have voting rights on platform upgrades and fee structures.
