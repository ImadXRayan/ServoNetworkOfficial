# Servo Network Whitepaper

## 1. Introduction
The gig economy and freelance marketplaces are growing rapidly, but traditional platforms (like Upwork or Fiverr) suffer from high fees (often 20%+), delayed payouts, and centralized control over user funds and accounts. Servo Network aims to disrupt this model using Web3 technology.

## 2. The Problem
- **High Fees:** Middlemen take a huge cut of the freelancer's earnings.
- **Payment Delays:** Cross-border payments can take days and incur heavy banking fees.
- **Lack of Trust:** Buyers fear non-delivery, and sellers fear non-payment.
- **Centralization:** Platforms can arbitrarily ban users or freeze funds.

## 3. The Servo Solution
Servo Network is a decentralized marketplace built on the Polygon blockchain. 
- **Zero to Low Fees:** By removing the corporate middleman and utilizing Polygon's low-gas infrastructure, fees are drastically reduced.
- **Instant Global Payouts:** Crypto payments settle in seconds, anywhere in the world.
- **Smart Contract Escrow:** Trust is enforced by code, not by a corporation.
- **Native Economy:** Driven by the SVO token, which aligns the incentives of all platform participants.

## 4. Technical Architecture
- **Frontend:** Modern, responsive React application.
- **Backend:** Decentralized infrastructure and Supabase for off-chain metadata.
- **Smart Contracts:** Solidity-based contracts deployed on Polygon for Token, Escrow, Staking, and Presale operations.

## 5. Roadmap
- **Phase 1:** Token generation (SVO), Presale, and Airdrop launch.
- **Phase 2:** Marketplace MVP launch, basic Escrow integration.
- **Phase 3:** Staking mechanisms and advanced dispute resolution.
- **Phase 4:** Full decentralization (DAO) and community governance.

## 6. Conclusion
Servo Network empowers freelancers and clients by giving them back control over their money, data, and agreements. Welcome to the future of work.
